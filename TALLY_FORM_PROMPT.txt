PIXELALTY CLIENT INTAKE FORM

Create a professional multi-page intake form for a digital service called Pixelalty. Pixelalty creates a $499 real-estate listing marketing video from photos and information supplied by the realtor/agent/customer.

The form is ONLY for customers who have already paid.

Use a progress bar. Keep the design clean and professional. Make required fields clearly required. Add a short introduction explaining that production begins after all required usable materials are received.

PAGE 1 — ORDER / CLIENT DETAILS
- Full name (required)
- Email address (required)
- Phone number (required)
- Real estate agency / brokerage name (required)
- Property address (required)
- Listing URL (required if available; otherwise allow “not yet live”)
- MLS number (optional)

PAGE 2 — PROPERTY INFORMATION
- Listing price (required)
- Bedrooms (required)
- Bathrooms (required)
- Square footage (required)
- Property type (single family, condo, townhome, land, other)
- Key selling features (long text, required)
- Important property details or disclaimers that must appear (long text, optional)
- Exact agent name/title to display (optional)
- Agent phone number to display (optional)
- Agent email to display (optional)
- Brokerage name to display (optional)

PAGE 3 — CREATIVE DIRECTION
- Desired style: Luxury / Modern / Energetic / Minimal / Warm / Other
- Primary platform: Instagram Reels / Facebook / YouTube Shorts / Website / Other
- Preferred orientation: Vertical 9:16 / Horizontal 16:9 / No preference
- Desired approximate length: 30 sec / 45 sec / 60 sec / No preference
- Specific text, slogans, or call-to-action to include (long text)
- Music preference: Upbeat / Luxury / Cinematic / Minimal / No preference
- Special instructions (long text)

PAGE 4 — FILE UPLOADS
Add file-upload fields:
- Listing photos (required, multiple files, image formats)
- Agent or brokerage logo (optional)
- Agent headshot (optional)
- Other brand assets or reference files (optional)

Add a note: Free Tally accounts limit each individual uploaded file to 10 MB. If a source file is larger, the customer should provide a private Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, or other authorized download link in the next field.
- External asset folder / download link (optional)

PAGE 5 — RIGHTS / AUTHORIZATION
Required checkbox:
“I confirm that I own the materials I am submitting or have permission from the applicable photographer, brokerage, homeowner, MLS provider, or other rights holder to use them for this project, and I authorize Pixelalty to process and edit them to create my commissioned listing video.”

Required checkbox:
“I confirm that the property facts, pricing, contact information, disclosures, and other information I supplied are accurate to the best of my knowledge.”

Required checkbox:
“I understand that the $499 package includes one consolidated revision round and that major scope changes or additional revisions may require a separate quote.”

Required checkbox:
“I have reviewed and agree to Pixelalty’s Terms of Service and Refund Policy at https://pixelalty.com/terms.html and https://pixelalty.com/refund-policy.html.”

PAGE 6 — FINAL REVIEW
Display a short summary reminding the customer:
- Pixelalty will review the submission.
- Production starts after complete usable materials are received.
- Pixelalty may contact them if files or information are missing.
- One revision round is included.
- Final files are delivered digitally through a private link.

Final submit button text: “Submit Project Materials”

THANK YOU PAGE
Title: “Your materials are in.”
Message: “Pixelalty has received your project information. Keep an eye on the email/phone information you supplied in case we need clarification. Please retain your Stripe receipt for your records.”

Turn on self-email notifications for new submissions.
Do not require respondents to create a Tally account.
