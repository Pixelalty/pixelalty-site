Privacy Policy

Effective September 19, 2026.

Privacy summary: Pixelalty collects only information reasonably needed to process orders, communicate with customers, produce the requested video, and maintain business records.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the site, Pixelalty may receive your name, email address, phone number, business or brokerage name, property address, listing URL, project instructions, uploaded photographs, logos, and other files you choose to provide.

2. Payment information

Payments are processed by Stripe. Pixelalty does not need to store your full payment-card number on this website. Stripe processes payment information under its own privacy and security practices.

3. Intake and uploaded files

Pixelalty may use a third-party form and file-upload provider to collect project materials. Information submitted through that service is also subject to that provider's privacy and security practices.

4. How information is used

Information may be used to fulfill the requested service, communicate about the project, process and reconcile payments, prevent abuse, maintain business records, handle support or disputes, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

5. Sharing

Pixelalty does not sell customer project information. Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to process payments, host the website, collect files, produce the requested work, deliver files, or comply with law.

6. Retention

Project information and files may be retained for a reasonable period for delivery, support, recordkeeping, dispute handling, and repeat-client convenience. Customers should keep their own copies of final deliverables and original source materials.

7. Security

Pixelalty uses third-party services and reasonable operational practices to reduce risk, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the official Pixelalty Instagram account at @pixelalty until an official business email is published.
