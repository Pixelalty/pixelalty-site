Refund Policy

Effective September 19, 2026.

Please read before ordering: Pixelalty sells custom creative work that begins after receiving payment and usable client materials.

Before production begins

If you request cancellation before Pixelalty has begun substantive production work, contact Pixelalty promptly. A refund may be provided when appropriate, subject to payment-processing limitations and applicable law.

After production begins

Because the service is customized to a specific property and customer, payments are generally non-refundable after substantive production has begun, except where required by law or where Pixelalty is unable to provide the purchased service.

Revisions are not refunds

The package includes one consolidated revision round. A reasonable request that falls within the original scope should normally be addressed through the included revision process rather than cancellation.

Customer delays or unusable materials

If a customer does not provide required materials, does not have permission to use supplied content, or provides materials that cannot reasonably be used, Pixelalty may pause the project while requesting replacements. Refund eligibility will depend on the amount of work already performed, the circumstances, and applicable law.

Duplicate or mistaken payments

If you believe you paid twice or made a payment in error, contact Pixelalty promptly with the Stripe receipt information so the transaction can be reviewed.

Chargebacks and disputes

Customers are encouraged to contact Pixelalty first so legitimate project or billing issues can be reviewed and resolved. Nothing in this policy limits rights that cannot legally be waived.

Contact

Refund or cancellation requests can be sent through the official Pixelalty Instagram account at @pixelalty until an official business email is published.
