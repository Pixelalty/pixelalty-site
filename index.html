FAQ

Before you order.

Do you photograph or film the property?

No. The $499 package is created remotely from the photos, listing information, and branding assets you provide.

Can I request changes?

Yes. One consolidated revision round is included. Additional changes or a major change in project scope may require a separate quote.

Who must own the listing photos?

You must own the supplied materials or have permission from the rights holder to use them for the project and authorize Pixelalty to process them for the commissioned video.

How will I receive the video?

Final deliverables are provided digitally through a private download link after the review process is complete.

When does production begin?

Production begins after payment and after Pixelalty receives the required project information and usable assets.

Does Pixelalty guarantee views, leads, or a sale?

No. Pixelalty provides a creative marketing deliverable. Advertising performance, engagement, leads, and property sales depend on many factors outside the scope of the service.
